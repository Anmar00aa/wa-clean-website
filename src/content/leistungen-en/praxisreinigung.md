In a medical practice, cleanliness shapes how patients feel. Waiting rooms, reception and washrooms are used heavily every day – and should still look well-kept at all times.

We clean your practice according to a fixed schedule agreed with you, usually outside consultation hours, so that your practice runs undisturbed. We pay particular attention to frequently touched surfaces and washrooms.

Which rooms are cleaned how often and with which products is agreed together with you – in line with your hygiene plan. Reprocessing of medical devices and instruments is not part of our services and remains with your practice team.
