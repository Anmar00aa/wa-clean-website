Beyond cleaning, buildings often need additional attention: inspection rounds, small repairs, or the upkeep of outdoor areas. With our caretaker service, we bundle these tasks with a single, consistent point of contact.

Regular inspection rounds help identify issues early, before they turn into larger damage. We handle minor repairs directly ourselves; for larger matters, we coordinate with suitable specialist tradespeople.

The service combines well with routine cleaning, garden maintenance or winter service, so you get all the services around your building from a single provider.
