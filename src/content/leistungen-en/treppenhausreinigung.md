A clean stairwell is one of the first impressions residents and visitors get of a building. We take care of regular cleaning according to the schedule set out in the house rules, or by individual arrangement with the property manager.

Alongside the stairs themselves, handrails, banisters, mailbox units and entrance areas are also part of the service. For property managers with multiple buildings, we coordinate all appointments centrally.

On request, we document the cleanings carried out, so owners' associations have clear proof of the service delivered.
