In childcare centres and schools, cleanliness is directly linked to the health of children and staff. We thoroughly clean group and classrooms, restrooms, hallways and gymnasiums, reliably and outside childcare and teaching hours.

We pay particular attention to hygiene in restroom areas as well as surfaces frequently touched by children. The cleaning schedule is agreed firmly with the facility or its operator.

For public operators and facilities with multiple locations, we're also available for cooperation as part of tender processes.
