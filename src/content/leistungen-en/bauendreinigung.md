After construction work, what's left behind is usually more than visible dirt: fine construction dust settles into every crevice, along with paint, silicone or adhesive residue on floors and tiles. Post-construction cleaning ensures the rooms can be handed over move-in ready.

We clean after new builds as well as renovations and conversions – from individual rooms to entire buildings. The appointment is coordinated with your construction schedule or planned handover date, so everything is ready on time.

On request, we also take care of the final window cleaning, so no extra effort falls on you after the construction work is done.
