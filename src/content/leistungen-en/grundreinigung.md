Deep cleaning goes beyond routine upkeep. It comes into play when rooms haven't been professionally cleaned for a long time, before starting a regular cleaning schedule, or during a tenant changeover.

This also covers areas that are often skipped in everyday cleaning: grout, corners, radiators, or heavily used floor surfaces. The result is a condition that can then be maintained well with regular upkeep.

We agree on the exact scope with you individually after a brief on-site visit or a description of the rooms, so the quote matches the actual condition.
