Clear panes make a visible difference – both for the impression from outside and for the light entering your rooms. With the right technique and suitable cleaning agents, we achieve streak-free results, even on larger glass surfaces.

We clean individual windows as well as continuous glass facades, shop windows and display cases. Frames, sills and reveals are cleaned on request, so the overall impression is right.

Window cleaning can be booked as a one-off job or on a fixed schedule – for example twice a year or quarterly, adapted to location and weather.
